{%- comment -%} Prints the URL of the page with the given `ref` in the current page language, e.g. the privacy policy: /confidentialite/ or /en/privacy/. Usage: Params: ref, lang (optional, defaults to page.lang). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ref_lang = include.lang | default: page.lang | default: "fr" -%} {%- assign ref_page = site.pages | where: "ref", include.ref | where: "lang", ref_lang | first -%} {{- site.baseurl }}{{ ref_page.url -}}
